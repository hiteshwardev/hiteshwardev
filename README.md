## About Me

I am interested in computational and applied sciences, with a focus on understanding complex physical systems through mathematical modelling, numerical simulations, and scientific computing.

My interests span computational physics, photonics, non-Hermitian systems, topological phenomena, and the role of disorder in wave-based systems. I enjoy connecting theoretical ideas with numerical methods to investigate physical behaviour, analyse results, and develop reproducible computational approaches.

- **Photonics and Wave Physics:** Light–matter interactions, photonic structures, and wave propagation.
- **Disorder and Complex Systems:** Correlated disorder, stochastic processes, and their physical effects.
- **Scientific Data Analysis:** Statistical methods, data visualization, and computational interpretation.
